# Reused study assets

`images/concert.png` and `images/comedy.png` are the illustrative photos previously generated for this same Burque design project, copied from `date-line-motion/assets/images/`. No new external image downloads or organizer assets are used.

For the 1.1.0 long-feed fixture, these three existing category photos are intentionally reused across the 48 fictional listings. No new asset commission or outside image source is implied by the additional event names. This pass tests UX/content volume; the descriptions do not claim the photographs document actual events.

The workshop photo, dotted texture and Manrope font are consumed from the independent `kit/assets/` copy. Its asset provenance and license notices are retained.

`icons/*.svg` are the existing Radix Icons from the prior prototype. The builder embeds their SVG paths with `currentColor`. The original Radix license is retained in `kit/licenses/`. No new hand-drawn icons were introduced.
