At the worktable

A different kind of night out.

Carve a simple block, pull your first print, and settle into an easygoing evening around a shared studio table. Make something imperfect and entirely yours.

Your evening includes

  • All materials and tools
  • Guidance from a local printmaker
  • One wine or alcohol-free pour
  • Your finished prints to take home

Good to know

Beginners are welcome. Wear something you’d happily get ink on. Come on your own or bring a friend; everyone shares a worktable.

This is a 21+ evening. Arrive about 10 minutes early to get settled.
