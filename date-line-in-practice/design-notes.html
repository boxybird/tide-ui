Date Line from Quiet Assembly 01 is now the selected favorite overall. Its original files remain untouched. This collection copies its approved frame source and styles, then introduces two deliberately narrow changes.

What is preserved

Manrope, weights, sizes, line heights, inset, card spacing, date hierarchy, dot texture, dark palette, warm action, full-screen event opening and rich scrolling details. The preserved baseline is always available for comparison. Source hashes record the approved files; the original project remains in its sibling folder.

01 / Local Partner

One clearly labelled advertisement follows the second event. It uses a quieter surface, a smaller headline, an advertiser name and an ordinary text link. It has no date, category or bookmark, so it reads as advertising rather than an organic event. The original event cards retain their dimensions and styling; the later card simply moves down by the reserved slot.

The event’s opening viewport is unchanged. A second local-partner ad appears after the workshop introduction, below the fold. Open that placement directly. Its separate border and Advertisement label distinguish it from event information. Ad links open a fictional local partner page.

The feed reserves a minimum 144px for this example instead of inserting content over the cards. This is a placement study, not a production recommendation for ad frequency. There is no ad network, tracking, rotating creative, real offer or monetization integration.

02 / Rough Upload

The card list is identical to the approved baseline. Only the opening photograph is replaced: crooked framing, an ugly flash hotspot, too much empty wall, mixed indoor light and blurred hands. It is a genuine visual stress fixture, not the polished workshop image with a decorative filter. The generated file is 1024×1536; the intended defects are composition, lighting and blur rather than a claim that it is a tiny-resolution upload.

The photograph is displayed with the original full-screen crop and overlay. Text, controls and actions stay in the same positions. The image remains visibly imperfect; the app’s readable hierarchy does not depend on flattering photography. A photo credit appears only in the longer listing. Quality detection, automatic subject selection and upload editing are future product decisions, not simulated here.
