Seating & entry

Primarily standing-room general admission, with limited seating available first come, first served. The venue does not allow re-entry.

Access arrangements

Contact the venue before your visit about step-free entry, seating and other access needs. Its FAQ directs patrons needing assistance to the manager on arrival; it does not confirm specific access facilities.

Check each event

Age restrictions, admission and doors/show times belong to the individual event. They are not a single venue-wide rule.
