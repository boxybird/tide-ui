At the worktable

An idea, a table and a little ink.

Drop in with scraps, sketches or nothing at all. The shared table is open for collage, simple stamps and a few hours of making things together.

Your evening includes

  • Shared tools, ink and paper
  • Guidance from a local printmaker
  • Your finished work to take home

Good to know

No previous printmaking experience is needed. Wear something you would be happy to get ink on.

All ages are welcome; younger makers should come with an adult.
