The rules that hold it together

Extensions inherit the visual structure of the original.

  1. 24px page insets. Cards meet edge to edge, with fine dividing rules.
  2. Dots belong to event surfaces. Forms and reading areas stay calm and plain.
  3. One primary accent per task. Secondary actions use an outline or a text link.
  4. 48px form controls; 44px icon targets. Radius: 2px fields, 2px actions, 4px documentation panels.
  5. Visible labels and focus rings. Meaningful status words accompany every status color.
  6. Long titles wrap. Missing facts stay unknown. A poor photo never carries the reading hierarchy.

Build next

Refine discovery and the attendance handoff first. Then the organizer submission and correction loop. Those journeys make the minimal interface useful and keep the listings trustworthy.

Decisions to keep open

External tickets or native RSVP? Who reviews submissions? How does an organizer edit an event? Those choices determine the next screens. An account wall and a permanent bottom navigation can wait.
