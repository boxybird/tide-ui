One new direction.
Six small refinements.

Still Hours is the second refinement family for Ink After Dark. Quiet Studies remains intact. The same three events, workshop photo and fuller listing make it possible to judge the design changes directly.

How this family differs

The first family used a compact utility header, separated dotted panels, mostly sans-serif names and a filled warm action. Still Hours moves toward a small evening journal: a larger weekend heading, a narrow date rail, uninterrupted full-width cards, warm charcoal, softly drawn serif names and an outlined action beside the price. The underlying mobile list and full-screen photographed event remain the same.

The six members hold that new composition constant. They vary in serif construction, weight, optical size, reading face, 24px or 28px shared edges, and closely related mineral colors. They are close alternatives within one family.

Type, with optical judgment

Fraunces anchors three studies; its softness varies modestly, with the optional wonky alternates switched off. Lora and Literata offer two useful contrasts while retaining the serif voice. Practical information remains in a sans serif. Titles use real variable weights, and synthetic bolding is disabled.

Sizes are normalized by visual weight rather than forced to match numerically. Card names sit on 32px lines at 25–26px. The opened title sits on 44px or 48px lines at 42–46px, with a deliberate break after “Ink After.” Body copy uses 14px/24px, or 16px/24px for Source Sans 3. Labels use 11px/16px; metadata uses 13px/20px on cards and 14px/20px in the detail facts. Expanded tracking stays in labels. Running text keeps natural spacing.

The type research came from the designers’ own material: Undercase Type’s Fraunces repository documents its variable axes; Cyreal’s Lora page describes its calligraphic construction; TypeTogether’s Literata page describes its screen-reading origins. These are type references; the event layouts are original design studies.

A small, coherent spacing system

The system uses 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40 and 48px steps. Related lines stay close; separate decisions receive larger pauses. The same outer inset aligns the header, card dates, opened title, facts, action and long-form content. Inside cards, a 40px date rail and 16px gutter give the event names a consistent reading edge.

Cards grow when names wrap. At 320px, Desert Frequencies moves to two lines without truncation or a smaller type exception. Facts use two flexible columns, with the venue on its own row. On short viewports, the event can grow slightly beyond one screen so its text and action remain intact. Fine rule thicknesses are optical separators and sit outside the spacing scale.

Darkness with clear hierarchy

Warm off-white carries names, softened neutrals carry supporting information, and a muted sage or mineral accent connects categories, prices and the outline action. Color does not carry meaning alone. The diagonal surface texture is a very low-contrast CSS treatment, so it needs no uploaded asset.

The first photo text always begins on a minimum 94% warm-ink scrim anchored to the content padding. This makes readability independent of the height of the phone or the brightness of a submitted photo. The photo still fills the whole opening surface, and the upper part keeps its unretouched character.

Seven targeted text combinations per study pass 4.5:1. The lowest is 7.10:1, including a conservative white-photo-pixel check that ignores the additional global photo shade. These checks cover primary, secondary, label, accent, footer and photo text; they do not constitute a full accessibility certification. See the W3C text-contrast guidance.

What the HTML does

Each pair contains two independent HTML documents. The Ink After Dark card opens its event page; Events returns to the list. Plan this evening and More about the workshop scroll to the richer details: description, materials, schedule, practical guidance and arrival information. Bookmark marks, Saved, and the other two event cards are visual references.

There is no browser JavaScript. Fonts, photo and Radix SVG icons are local, with licenses bundled. Real links have visible keyboard focus; phone controls are at least 44px high, and the main action is 48px. A reduced-motion preference removes smooth scrolling. A higher-contrast preference strengthens text and removes the card texture.
