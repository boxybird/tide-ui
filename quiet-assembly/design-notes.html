Keep the foundation.
Bring the date forward.

Quiet Studies is the primary reference. These three retain its sans serif families, compact header, category/title/venue hierarchy, subdued dot texture and full-screen photographed event. The cards now touch with fine separators, and the date has a clearer role.

Three date treatments

01 Date Line / Manrope

The category, event name and venue stay on the original left edge. A larger day number gives the existing information line more presence without reorganizing the card.

02 Date Corner / DM Sans

A quiet date block occupies the upper right. The original title and venue hierarchy remains on the left; time, price and bookmark move into one calm bottom line.

03 Date Rail / Instrument Sans

The second family’s date rail returns in a plain sans serif. Category, title, venue and time keep the first family’s order. A slightly wider inset gives this version more air.

The opened event stays immersive

The first event viewport uses the same full-bleed workshop photo, single-line title, vertical facts and filled action as Quiet Studies. The longer description, inclusions, schedule and practical details begin below that first viewport, so scrolling remains part of the experience.

Ink After Dark opens by an ordinary HTML link. The close control returns to the list. The action and detail cue scroll to the richer listing. Bookmark marks, Saved and the first two event cards are visual references in this static study.

A shared spacing system

The 4px scale, 24px or 28px outer inset and explicit line heights come from the first family. Cards have zero gaps; a thin rule supplies separation. Date sizes are 26px or 32px, while names stay at 24px and opened titles at 36px. Dates receive prominence through size and position without taking over the title hierarchy. Text can wrap rather than truncate.

Typography and color

Manrope, DM Sans and Instrument Sans are the exact local fonts used in Quiet Studies. Every screen and the comparison canvas uses sans serif type. The original cool dark surfaces, off-white names, secondary neutrals and restrained warm accent remain.

The original photo overlay stays intact at the reference phone height. On short phones, a content-anchored scrim protects the first text line. A higher-contrast CSS branch strengthens text; reduced-motion preferences remove smooth scrolling.
