# Research — Gather, amplified

Inspected 2026-10-21. The request was to push microinteractions beyond the previous restrained Gather pass, while retaining Date Line's resting design.

1. [Magnetic Buttons, Manoela Ilic / Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2020/08/05/magnetic-buttons/) and its [live demo](https://tympanus.net/Development/MagneticButtons/). Read the article and opened the live visual reference. Its useful idea is independent motion of button, glyph and surrounding layer. Adaptation: close control magnetic translation, counter-scaled/rotating glyph and delayed outer ring. No source code copied and no magnetic hover observation claimed: this browser tool does not expose pointer-hover input.
2. [Chevron morph, Juan Di Nella](https://microinteractions.dev/chevron-morph/). Opened the live page, scrubbed its inspector to 100ms and visually examined the arrow state. It gives navigation a directional character. Adaptation: closing X and destination arrow are matched native snapshots in a return token. Our crossfade and arcing return are original choreography, not the reference's chevron-path technique.
3. [Plucked underline, Juan Di Nella](https://microinteractions.dev/plucked-underline/). Read its timing and damped-release study. Adaptation: card-edge release pulse and damped landing; we do not reproduce its underline geometry.
4. [Line & Letter Hover Animations, Codrops](https://tympanus.net/Development/LineTypeEffect/). Read as a typography-motion reference. We retained whole-title movement rather than per-letter DOM changes so the approved typography, kerning and accessible title stay intact.
5. [Chrome: same-document view transitions](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/view-transitions/same-document). Verified the named-snapshot model and custom pseudo-element animation approach. Close and return anchor have one unique return-control name per rendered state; the animation extends native geometry with translate/rotate/scale, rather than animating a duplicate DOM screenshot.

Motion decisions: one large return story, not unrelated decoration. Warm token appears only during close. No permanent extra card affordance or extra text. Expressive explores a plausible rich treatment; Overdrive deliberately stretches amplitude and timing to expose the upper limit. Defaults are experiments, not a claim of universal UX best practice.
